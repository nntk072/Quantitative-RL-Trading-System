# Research evidence

Full machine outputs live here. Human interpretation, experimental design, and
limitations belong in [`docs/research/`](../../docs/research/README.md). Name
new evidence by its research subject or question; keep dates, run IDs, and job
IDs in manifests and metadata. Immutable historical artifacts may retain their
original names when that is necessary to preserve exact provenance.

Historical source mappings and retained source hashes are indexed in the
[historical source provenance packet](historical_source_provenance/README.md).
Archived run artifacts remain checksum-preserved outside the repository; they
are not current research results or an active work queue.

- [Entry-idea opportunity counts](entry_idea_opportunity_counts/entry_idea_opportunity_counts.md)
  and its annual/timeframe CSV tables. Counts describe cached contexts, not
  executed trades.
- [Training-only trade-setup opportunity census](trade_setup_opportunity_census/README.md)
  records configured source-time identities and cross-timeframe alignment; its
  M1 FVG ledger retains overlapping detector confirmations separately from the
  scalar feature projection. These counts are not policy actions or trades.
- [Monthly entry and exit attribution](entry_and_exit_attribution/README.md)
  is withdrawn historical evidence; its result summary is a provenance stub
  and its paired-trade inputs are archive-only.
- [Selected policy trade lifecycle](selected_policy_trade_lifecycle/README.md)
  is withdrawn historical performance evidence. Its JSON reports are
  provenance stubs; row-level records remain archived. The
  [trade excursion packet](selected_policy_trade_lifecycle/trade_excursions/README.md)
  follows the same withdrawn status.
- [Daily-breach full-evaluation failure](simulator_execution_parity/daily_breach_full_evaluation_failure/README.md)
  verifies that a daily-floor breach blocks later-session entries in both the
  training environment and event backtest; the historical recovery trace is
  retained separately and marked superseded.
- [Saved A4 equity reconciliation](simulator_execution_parity/equity_series_reconciliation/README.md)
  is withdrawn historical risk/performance evidence; current summary JSONs are
  provenance stubs and source identities remain archived.
- [Selected checkpoint training replay](selected_checkpoint_train_replay/README.md)
  is withdrawn historical evidence. Its JSON summary is a source/run identity
  stub; external replay reports remain archive-only.
- [Exit-action semantics](exit_action_semantics/README.md)
  preserves the frozen decoder comparison, selected cohort, and conditional
  fixed-entry shadow under a semantic packet with source checksums.
- [Setup-conditioned replay output](setup_conditioned_reasoning_replay/README.md)
  with train/test summaries, plots, and policy diagnostic JSON.
- [Entry-idea feature previews](entry_idea_context_previews/README.md): H1
  structural context figures and source metadata, not trade-performance results.
- [Directional swing cache validation](directional_swing_cache_validation.json):
  machine-readable cache validation record.
- [Configured multi-timeframe data alignment](releases/negative-evidence-readiness/configured_timeframe_parity.json):
  native/M1 timestamp and OHLCV parity across both local periods, with native
  spread differences and schedule limits kept explicit.
- [Fill-to-tick quote source alignment](fill_quote_source_alignment/README.md):
  selected treatment/control fills aligned against one streamed local tick
  export, with side carry, missing quotes, tied timestamps, and input hashes
  recorded without asserting within-tie chronology.
- [Feature causality and availability inventory](feature_causality/README.md):
  generated source-function and setup-flag matrix with unassessed timing
  fields made explicit.
- [Model observation and action evidence](model_observation_and_action_semantics/README.md):
  source/configuration account of historical streams, candidate masks, and
  policy action conditioning; it makes no economic performance claim.
- [Training candidate proposals](candidate_proposals/README.md) and their
  [local quote coverage](candidate_outcome_coverage/README.md) preserve the
  static proposal denominator and interval-level fill/exit data limitations.
  They do not establish account eligibility or realized policy outcomes.
- [Candidate signal validity semantics](candidate_validity_semantics/README.md)
  records the selected-profile PO3, IFVG, and standard-deviation lifetimes and
  explains why the common proposal TTL cannot be read as policy execution.
- [Candidate conditional outcomes](candidate_outcomes/README.md) records the
  complete training-only proposal/bracket replay, unresolved-data reasons, and
  verified disposition table/figure. The rows are conditional bracket
  combinations, not independent trades or policy outcomes; quote coverage and
  account-state limitations remain explicit.
- [Candidate action linkage](candidate_action_linkage/README.md) records exact
  event and proposal-window overlap in the matched development baseline traces.
  It does not link historical PPO actions or identify a selected market/limit
  proposal, bracket, fill, or policy value.
- [Negative-result release readiness snapshot](releases/negative-evidence-readiness/README.md):
  source-pinned internal handoff with complete local data checksums; still
  blocked by current-source quote/calendar findings and incomplete scientific
  and software release gates. Provider/license research was excluded by user
  direction; the snapshot includes no raw market data or rights claim.
- [Selected-pair result identity](releases/negative-evidence-readiness/selected_pair_viability_manifest.json):
  v2 withdrawal record retaining archived run identities and source hashes;
  it contains no current outcome metrics or performance claim.
- [Historical variant-screen artifact recoverability](historical_variant_screen_recoverability/README.md):
  report-pack availability for a superseded 18-variant screen, retained for
  retrospective disposition only and excluded from model selection.
