# Candidate proposal quote coverage

For market proposals, this packet checks the eligible action-fill minute. For pending limits, it checks every minute through the earlier of proposal expiry, session close, and training cutoff. Incomplete intervals cannot support a local no-touch expiry label. A passing interval remains conditional local-feed evidence and is not proof of venue-complete quotes or policy action.

Input packet identities and counts are in `coverage_manifest.json`; per-proposal intervals are in `proposal_interval_coverage.csv`.
