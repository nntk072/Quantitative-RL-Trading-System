# Exit-action semantics evidence

This packet preserves the recorded frozen-checkpoint decoder comparison and
the selected common-entry and fixed-entry shadow evidence under a stable
research concept. The evidence manifest maps every retained asset to its
historical source path and SHA-256, and records the checksum of each semantic
copy.

The principal interpretation is in
[`decoder_behavior_comparison.md`](decoder_behavior_comparison.md). The
tracked cohort notebook reproduces the 53-row common-entry summary and its
monthly figure. The fixed-entry shadow files preserve a separate conditional
trade-level analysis; they do not form a compounded strategy backtest.

## Evidence boundary

- The v2/v3 comparison uses frozen checkpoints and the declared training
  partition. It does not show that either decoder has an economic edge.
- The exact common-entry cohort is a small, post-divergence-selected subset;
  it does not isolate decoder effects causally.
- The fixed-entry shadow is conditional trade-level evidence. Its documented
  execution and account-path exceptions remain part of its interpretation.
- The original full per-arm replay inputs, raw ticks, and checkpoints are not
  included in this tracked packet. The packet supports its declared R2 cohort
  recomputation and preserves the historical output lineage, not a full replay.
- The original proposal remains in the external historical backup recorded by
  the migration manifest. No training or exposed-period result is added here.

[`docs/research/exit_action_semantics.md`](../../../docs/research/exit_action_semantics.md),
and the reproducible cohort calculation is in
[`docs/reproduce/exit_action_semantics.ipynb`](../../../docs/reproduce/exit_action_semantics.ipynb).
