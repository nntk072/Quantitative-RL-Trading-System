# Thesis figure and table registry

This registry maps every planned thesis item to current source evidence and
states whether it can support a figure or table. It does not promote historical
plots into current matched results. Current tracked-file SHA-256 values are
maintained in [`RELEASE_MANIFEST.json`](RELEASE_MANIFEST.json); local input
hashes and aliases are maintained in
[`RAW_DATA_SHA256_MANIFEST.json`](RAW_DATA_SHA256_MANIFEST.json). Dates below
identify evidence exposure, not public artifact names.

## Reproduction grades

- **R2 bounded** means a result can be recomputed from retained rows or current
  local inputs within the stated scope. It does not imply checkpoint replay or
  independent replication.
- **R1 historical** means a retained report/plot is linked to a historical
  run, but its complete source records or current-code regeneration path are
  not established.
- **Blocked** means the required comparable or authoritative evidence is not
  available. **Not applicable yet** means the analysis depends on earlier
  scientific gates that have not passed.

documentation diagrams and notebooks through the strict Sphinx command in the

assert that every planned result exists.
