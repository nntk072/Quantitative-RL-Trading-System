# Reading list

This is a curated reading list for methods used or discussed in the portal.
The bibliography in the supplied research archive is a discovery source; entries
below are cited for background and do not imply that a paper's method is
implemented or that its empirical conclusions transfer to this project.

## Reinforcement learning

- Sutton, R. S. and Barto, A. G. (2018). *Reinforcement Learning: An
  Introduction*, 2nd ed. [MIT Press edition](http://incompleteideas.net/book/the-book-2nd.html).
- Schulman, J. et al. (2017). “Proximal Policy Optimization Algorithms.”
  [arXiv:1707.06347](https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.06347).
- Haarnoja, T. et al. (2018). “Soft Actor-Critic: Off-Policy Maximum Entropy
  Deep Reinforcement Learning with a Stochastic Actor.”
  [PMLR paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v80/haarnoja18b.html).
- Achiam, J. et al. (2017). “Constrained Policy Optimization.”
  [PMLR paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v70/achiam17a.html).
- Kaelbling, L. P., Littman, M. L., and Cassandra, A. R. (1998). “Planning and
  Acting in Partially Observable Stochastic Domains.” *Artificial
  Intelligence*, 101(1–2), 99–134.

## Sequences and time series

- Bai, S., Kolter, J. Z., and Koltun, V. (2018). “An Empirical Evaluation of
  Generic Convolutional and Recurrent Networks for Sequence Modeling.”
  [arXiv:1803.01271](https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.01271).
- Vaswani, A. et al. (2017). “Attention Is All You Need.”
  [NeurIPS proceedings](https://papers.nips.cc/paper/7181-attention-is-all-you-need).
- Lim, B. et al. (2021). “Temporal Fusion Transformers for Interpretable
  Multi-horizon Time Series Forecasting.” *International Journal of
  Forecasting*, 37(4), 1748–1764.

## Evaluation and research selection

- Sharpe, W. F. (1966). “Mutual Fund Performance.” *The Journal of Business*,
  39(1), 119–138.
- Lo, A. W. (2002). “The Statistics of Sharpe Ratios.” *Financial Analysts
  Journal*, 58(4), 36–52.
- Bailey, D. H. and López de Prado, M. (2014). “The Deflated Sharpe Ratio.”
  *The Journal of Portfolio Management*, 40(5), 94–107.
- Bailey, D. H. et al. (2017). “The Probability of Backtest Overfitting.”
  *The Journal of Computational Finance*, 20(4), 39–69.
- White, H. (2000). “A Reality Check for Data Snooping.” *Econometrica*,
  68(5), 1097–1126.

## Trading and market data

- Lo, A. W., Mamaysky, H., and Wang, J. (2000). “Foundations of Technical
  Analysis: Computational Algorithms, Statistical Inference, and Empirical
  Implementation.” *The Journal of Finance*, 55(4), 1705–1765.
- Cont, R. (2001). “Empirical Properties of Asset Returns: Stylized Facts and
  Statistical Issues.” *Quantitative Finance*, 1(2), 223–236.
- Zhang, Z., Zohren, S., and Roberts, S. (2020). “Deep Reinforcement Learning
  for Trading.” *The Journal of Financial Data Science*, 2(2), 25–40.
