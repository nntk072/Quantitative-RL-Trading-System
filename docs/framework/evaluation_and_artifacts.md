# Evaluation and artifacts

Evaluation replays a provider against market data and records equity, trades, fills, risk events, and metrics. Artifacts identify the inputs and outputs needed to inspect a run.

See [evaluation tutorial](../learn/tutorials/05_evaluation.ipynb), [evaluation methodology](../research/evaluation_methodology), [artifact reference](../reference/artifacts), and [metrics reference](../reference/metrics). Research evidence and reproducibility levels are indexed under [Research](../research/README) and [Reproduce](../reproduce/index).
