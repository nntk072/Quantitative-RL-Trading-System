# Research integrity

The global causal, training-only fit, chronological validation, risk, and
locked-OOS invariants are in `AGENTS.md`. The active stage order and its gates
are in `docs/operations/research_campaign_plan.md`; do not bypass or silently
weaken them.

- No future outcome or post-decision value may enter policy inputs.
- Keep development, exposed reporting, and any genuinely untouched period
  labels distinct. Never select on exposed data.
- Survival qualification precedes portfolio optimization. Hard risk limits
  remain fixed; a valid breached run is a negative outcome, not an invalid run.
- Each reported number must resolve to a source-identified run, config, data,
  metric definition, period role, and checksummed artifacts.
- A smoke or short pilot verifies implementation only. It is not an economic
  result, training qualification, or profitability claim.
- Preserve negative, null, breached, no-trade, censored, invalid, and incomplete
  results. Never fabricate evidence or choose only successful paths.
